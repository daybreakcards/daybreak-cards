About us
How Daybreak got started

It started with a shoebox of cards from a decade of on-and-off collecting, and a spreadsheet that had stopped making sense. Weekends turned into local shows — buying, selling, chasing cards we just loved the art of. Daybreak Cards grew out of that, one folding table at a time.

Every card on this site has passed through our hands first — checked under good light, sleeved, and priced by us. No bot-run price feeds, no drop-shipped inventory.

We built Daybreak to make the show-table experience better — everything we didn't love about the other side of the table, done our own way.
